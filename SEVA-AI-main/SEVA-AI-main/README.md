## Project Overview

**SEVA AI** is an AI-powered citizen assistance platform designed to help people discover relevant government schemes and services through a simple, conversational interface. The main goal of the project is to reduce the difficulty citizens face while understanding government schemes, eligibility requirements, and required documents.
The system allows citizens to interact using **text or voice** in both **English and Kannada**. Voice input is converted into text using speech-to-text processing, after which the system understands the citizen's request and extracts important information from the conversation.
The platform collects essential details such as **name, age, income, occupation, state, district, category, and the citizen's problem**. Instead of forcing users to fill out a long form, the system follows a conversational approach. It identifies the information that is already available and automatically asks relevant follow-up questions for the missing details. This makes the interaction easier, especially for users who may not be comfortable with complex digital forms.
The collected information is maintained in a structured **Citizen Profile**. This profile acts as the common data format used by the different components of the system. No information is intentionally invented when a citizen has not provided it. Missing information is identified and requested through follow-up questions.
Once the required information is collected, the completed profile is passed to the **eligibility and scheme-matching system**. The eligibility engine evaluates the citizen's details against predefined scheme rules and identifies the government schemes or services for which the citizen may qualify.
The system also includes **document processing and OCR capabilities**. When a scheme requires supporting documents, users can upload the required documents. OCR is used to extract relevant information from these documents, which can then be used for verification and eligibility processing.
The application follows a modular architecture with a **React/Vite frontend**, **FastAPI backend services**, conversational AI and information extraction, speech-to-text processing, eligibility logic, and OCR-based document processing. The AI and voice service exposes APIs that allow the frontend to send text or audio and receive the AI response, structured citizen profile, missing fields, language, and next question.
For the prototype, the system focuses on fast and practical processing suitable for a hackathon environment. English and Kannada support makes the platform more accessible to local citizens, while the conversational workflow reduces the need for users to understand complicated government forms.
Overall, SEVA AI combines **conversational AI, multilingual interaction, voice processing, structured information extraction, eligibility checking, scheme matching, and document verification** into one platform. The objective is to provide a simpler and more accessible way for citizens to understand and access government welfare schemes and services.
